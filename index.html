OUR APPROACH

Most agencies lose what they learn about your brand. We built ours not to.

Cedar Point Media started from a simple frustration: every new hire, every new tool, every busy week meant re-explaining the same brand rules and repeating the same mistakes. So instead of relying on memory and good intentions, we built Cedar Intelligence — a governed system that remembers what's approved, what's been rejected, and why — so your account doesn't restart from zero every month.

The result is a small, senior team that moves fast without cutting corners, and a client relationship that gets sharper the longer we work together.

01

Brand-first, always

Every deliverable is checked against your approved visual and voice history before it reaches you.

02

Nothing ships without you

Content, campaigns and spend all require your sign-off. No surprises in your ad account or your feed.

03

We don't repeat mistakes

Every resolved issue becomes a rule that protects the account going forward.

QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

What platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business — whichever mix fits where your audience actually spends time.

Who approves content before it goes live?

You do. Cedar Intelligence drafts and checks work against your brand rules, but nothing publishes and no ad spends without your sign-off.

Can you work with our existing brand identity?

Yes — we start by learning your current visual and voice history so new content stays consistent from day one. If you don't have a brand system yet, we can build one.

How fast can we get started?

Most accounts kick off within a week of our strategy call — starting with a short discovery phase to lock in your brand DNA and goals.

Is my brand information kept separate from other clients?

Strictly. Every client's brand memory is isolated — it's never used as a reference or fallback for another account.
