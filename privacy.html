Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this policy covers

CedarPoint Media ("we," "us") runs this website to showcase our work and let visitors reach out about our services. This page explains, plainly, what happens with information you give us through it.

What we collect

The only information we receive is what you choose to type into a form on this site — for example the Free Audit request, the Request a Quote flow, or the Book a Consultation form. That can include your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, business name, and details about your project.

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics software, and it does not run its own server-side database. Submitting a form opens a pre-filled email (via Gmail) addressed to us — the information travels the same way a normal email would, through your own email provider.

How we use it

  • To reply to your audit request, quote request, or consultation booking.
  • To understand your project so we can give a relevant, useful answer instead of a generic one.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Since submissions arrive as ordinary email, they're retained in our inbox like any other correspondence, until we no longer need them or you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, or to delete it, by emailing consultingcedarpoint@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change — for example if we add real analytics or a booking system with its own database — we'll update this page to reflect that honestly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to consultingcedarpoint@gmail.com or +961 81 113 001.
