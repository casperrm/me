Our Story

Built for brands that want more than "posting content."

From product launches to fully bilingual content calendars, we shape every post around one question: will this actually move the needle for your brand?

  • Bilingual creative (English & Arabic)

Who We Are

We are CedarPoint Media.

We're a small, focused team that treats every client's brand like it's our own — because a bloated agency with account managers relaying messages between you and the people actually doing the work isn't what growing brands need.

What we bring is direct communication, bilingual creative that actually lands in both English and Arabic markets, and reporting you can read without a marketing degree. No inflated promises, no recycled templates — just strategy and content built around your brand specifically.

How We Work Day to Day

Our approach.

You talk directly to the people creating your content and running your ads — not an account manager relaying messages back and forth.

  • A dedicated point of contact via WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • Fast replies — usually within a few hours
  • Strategy check-ins, not just status updates

Every piece of content is planned, not improvised — and nothing goes live without your sign-off.

  • Monthly content calendar shared in advance
  • A clear approval step before anything posts
  • Revisions included, not billed as extras

You'll always know what's actually working — reported in plain language, not vanity numbers.

  • Monthly performance reports (reach, engagement, conversions)
  • Honest notes on what to change next, not just charts
  • A long-term roadmap, reviewed quarterly
